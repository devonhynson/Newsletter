NEWSLETTER STUDIO: USE IT ON YOUR OWN

Open it on your computer
Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser. No Claude account is needed.

Put it online (about 5 minutes)
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a link right away.
3. To use your own address, open the site in Netlify, go to Domain management, and add a subdomain such as newsletter.devonhynson.com.

Update it later
In Netlify, open the site, go to the Deploys tab, and drag the updated folder there. That keeps the same address.

Where your work is saved
Your draft saves in the browser you use, on that device. It is separate from any draft you made inside Claude, so the studio opens with the sample issue the first time. "Start blank" keeps your brand and footer settings.

Downloads
"Save PNG" on each social graphic and "Download .html" on the email work as normal browser downloads here.
