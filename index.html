Before you send

    Social posts

    1080 px wide

    Graphics are drawn from your issue. Feed size (4:5) fits Instagram, Facebook, Threads and LinkedIn; story size (9:16) fits Stories, Reels and TikTok covers. Post them together as a carousel; the flip card's front and back go in order so readers swipe for the answer.

    Save a graphic with its button, or right-click (long-press on a phone) the image.

    Text message

    Short text (SMS)
    Longer text (MMS, WhatsApp, group text)

    One SMS holds 160 plain characters, or 70 if it has emoji or curly quotes. Longer texts split into parts and cost more to send. Only text people who agreed to receive texts from you.

    Plain text

    Paste this into your email tool's plain-text version. It's also handy for a Substack note, a Facebook group post or a quick email to parents.

    
      
    

      
    
        
    Website flip cards
    
        
    Try them here: every card flips back and forth each time you tap it, as many times as you like. They work with a keyboard too, and stay still for visitors who turn off motion.
    
        
    
        
    For your Bolt site (no AI tokens)
    
        
    1. In Bolt's Code tab, make a new file in the components folder named NewsletterFlipCards.tsx and paste this code in. 2. On the page where the cards go, add import NewsletterFlipCards from "./components/NewsletterFlipCards"; at the top and <NewsletterFlipCards /> where they should appear. 3. Click Publish. When you change your cards here, copy again and paste over the old code.
    
        
    
        
    
        
    For other website builders
    
        
    Paste this into a custom HTML or embed block (Ghost, Squarespace, WordPress, Wix and similar). It needs no scripts, so it keeps flipping even when a builder strips them out.
